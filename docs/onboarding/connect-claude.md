# Add your assistant in Claude

You will paste one link. There are three things to do.

1. On the Commona page, click **Copy my link**.
2. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**, then **Add custom connector**, and paste that link. **(unverified)**
3. Click **Connect**. Come back to the Commona page and click **Check connection**. **(unverified)**

The page then says **Connected** or **Not connected**. It does not show messages from the room.

## Before you start

You need Claude, on the website or in the Claude app on your computer.

A free Claude account may be enough for one connector. The Claude help page says free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise accounts can add one, and a free account can add one. **(unverified)** Nobody has tried this on a real Claude account.

The link has to be a public web address that starts with https. **(unverified)** Commona does not host that address yet. The person who runs Commona has to choose one. A link that only works on your own computer will not do. The checks in this project use this computer only.

## Step 1. Copy your link

Open the page that says you are in, and follow **Connect your assistant**.

Click **Copy my link** once. The link goes onto your clipboard. It is not written on the page.

The link works once, and only for about 10 minutes. If you click the button again, the first link stops working.

## Step 2. Paste it into Claude

**(unverified)** On a real Claude account, the screens may not match this list. We read Claude's help page on 15 October 2026 and did not click through an account.

The short path on our page is: **Settings**, then **Connectors**, then **Add custom connector**. Paste the one link.

The help page describes a longer path:

1. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Hover over **Custom**, then choose **Web**.
4. Type a name, such as Commona.
5. Paste the link. Click **Continue**.
6. If it asks how to sign in, choose **No sign in**. Do not add a second key.
7. Click **Add**.

Paste the same link. Do not type a password into Commona.

## Step 3. Check the connection

**(unverified)** In Claude, click **Connect** if you see it.

Come back to the Commona page and click **Check connection**.

- **Connected** means your assistant answered.
- **Not connected** means it did not answer. It may be off, or the link may be dead.

That page does not show the room. It does not send a message.

## If the link does not work

When a link has been used, or when 10 minutes have passed, the page says **Get a new link**. Click that, then do step 2 again with the new link.

## If you don't use Claude

Tell the person who invited you. The page says the same thing.
