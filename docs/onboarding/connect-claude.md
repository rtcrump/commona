# Add your assistant in Claude

You will paste one link. There are three things to do.

1. On the Commona page, click **Copy my link**.
2. On [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or in Claude Desktop, open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, then **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**, and paste that link. **(unverified)**
3. After it is added, turn it on in each chat with **+**, then **Connectors**. Come back to the Commona page and click **Check connection**. **(unverified)**

The page then says **Connected** or **Not connected**. It does not show messages from the room.

## Before you start

Use Claude on the website, claude.ai, or the Claude app on your computer, Claude Desktop.

Use a personal account. Do not use a Team or Enterprise account. On those accounts, only an organization Owner can add a connector. **(unverified)**

A free plan can add only one custom connector. If you already have one, remove it before you add this one. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and remove the old one. **(unverified)**

The link has to be a public web address that starts with https. **(unverified)** Commona does not host that address yet. The person who runs Commona has to choose one. A link that only works on your own computer will not do. The checks in this project use this computer only.

## Step 1. Copy your link

Open the page that says you are in, and follow **Connect your assistant**.

Click **Copy my link** once. The link goes onto your clipboard. It is not written on the page.

Use that link in Claude within about 10 minutes. After Claude connects the first time, the same link stays in place, so Claude can connect again with it.

If you click the button again, you get a new link, and the earlier one stops working. If 10 minutes pass before Claude connects, the page says **Get a new link**.

## Step 2. Paste it into Claude

**(unverified)** On a real Claude account, the screens may not match this list. We read Claude's help pages on 15 October 2026 and did not click through an account.

On a personal account, on claude.ai or in Claude Desktop:

1. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
2. Click **+ Add**.
3. Click **Add custom connector**.
4. Type a name, such as Commona.
5. Paste the link.
6. Click **Add**.

The words "hover over Custom, then choose Web" are the Team path. A personal account does not use that path. **(unverified)**

If Claude asks how to sign in, one choice on the help page is **No sign in**. That choice is untested. Do not add a second key.

Paste the same link. Do not type a password into Commona.

## Step 3. Turn it on, then check

**(unverified)** After the connector is added, it is not on in a chat until you turn it on.

1. Open a chat.
2. Click **+**.
3. Click **Connectors**.
4. Turn on the connector you just added.

Do that in each chat where you want it. Once it is added, it also works in the Claude app on your phone. **(unverified)**

Come back to the Commona page and click **Check connection**.

- **Connected** means your assistant answered.
- **Not connected** means it did not answer. It may be off.

That page does not show the room. It does not send a message.

## If the link does not work

If Claude has not connected yet and the page says **Get a new link**, the first link ran out of time. Click that, then do step 2 again with the new link.

If Claude already connected and you want a different link, click **Get a new link**. The old one stops working. Paste the new one into Claude.

## If you don't use Claude

Tell the person who invited you. The page says the same thing.
