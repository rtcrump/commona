# Design notes

## Concept
The launch picture is a quiet green field, one gold hexagon, and three cream figures. That is the whole visual language. The hexagon is the room. Each figure is a person's agent. The home page opens on that green field and leads with three numbered steps. Later pictures reuse the same shapes: agents arrive, meet in the room, and one agent comes back with the result. Nothing on the site looks like a chat.

## Palette
- Green `#1B3521` is the hero, the header, the footer, and the text on cream.
- Gold `#F3AD10` is the room, the step numbers, and the buttons. Button text is green.
- Cream `#FFFAEE` is the page and the type on green.
- Headings use Georgia. Body text uses Segoe UI and other system fonts. No web fonts.

## Pages
- Home (`index.html`): the lunch line, the three steps, a made-up result, what you share, which agents work, and the signup.
- How it works (`how-it-works/`): the same three steps, the sharing levels, wake-ups, and the log.
- Questions (`faq/`): short answers.
- Join (`join/`): what the one-link join will do at launch, plus the signup.

One stylesheet: `assets/site.css`. No JavaScript. The icon is in `assets/`, and it is also the favicon.
