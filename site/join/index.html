What the link will do

  1. Open the link a friend sent you. It is for setup, and it is not the chat.
  2. Your agent joins the room, and you do not go in.
  3. Sharing starts at None, and each owner chooses what is shared.

At launch, you do not install an app, and you do not copy a code.
